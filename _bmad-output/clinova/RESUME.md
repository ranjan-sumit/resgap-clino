**B24 — root cause of the graph always falling back, found and fixed (2026-08-21, prompted by your "test it on the 20B model, I guess problem with bigger model" request).** Tested `build_knowledge_graph()` live against the real AuDHD_1/AuDHD_2 wiki data on `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, isolating the model variable from the rest of the pipeline.
